☕ Gyaan Ki Baat: WebSockets vs. SSE vs. gRPC in 60 Seconds ⚡📡
Why did ChatGPT choose SSE, WhatsApp choose WebSockets, and Uber choose gRPC?
Stop defaulting to WebSockets for everything! Here's the cheat sheet:

📻 Server-Sent Events (SSE): One-way pipe (Server ➔ Client). Perfect for ChatGPT token streaming, stock tickers, live scores. Native auto-reconnect via `EventSource`!
📞 WebSockets: Full-duplex 2-way call (Client ⇄ Server). Perfect for WhatsApp chat, Figma multiplayer, live gaming.
🚀 gRPC: Binary Protobuf over HTTP/2 for microservices. Up to 7x-10x faster serialization than JSON!

💡 TL;DR: If data only flows one way (Server to Client), use SSE. It's 10x simpler and handles reconnects natively! 🧠
