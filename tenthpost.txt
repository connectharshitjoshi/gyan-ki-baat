☕ Gyaan Ki Baat #10 — Real-Time Protocols: WebSockets vs. SSE vs. gRPC ⚡📡

Ever wondered why ChatGPT streams text word-by-word, WhatsApp sends instant replies, and Uber matches cabs in milliseconds? 🚗👇

They all use different real-time protocols tailored to their use case. Here is the simple 2-minute breakdown:

---

1️⃣ Long Polling (The Impatient Passenger) 🚗
• Client: "New messages yet? No. New messages yet? No."
• Repetitive HTTP headers on every loop. High latency, heavy overhead.

2️⃣ Server-Sent Events (The Live Radio Broadcast) 📻
• One-way continuous pipe from Server ➔ Client (`text/event-stream`).
• Why ChatGPT uses SSE: You send ONE prompt, and the AI streams 150 words back token-by-token. No need for heavy 2-way WebSockets!

3️⃣ WebSockets (The 2-Way Phone Call) 📞
• Full-duplex bidirectional TCP socket. Both client and server can send data anytime.
• Why WhatsApp & Figma use WebSockets: Ultra-low framing overhead (2-6 bytes) and sub-20ms live collaboration.

4️⃣ gRPC (The Internal Rocket) 🚀
• Used between backend microservices (like Uber & Netflix).
• Uses binary Protocol Buffers (Protobuf) over HTTP/2 instead of heavy JSON text strings (7x-10x faster serialization!).

---

💡 Gyaan Ki Baat:
Don't default to WebSockets for everything! If your data flow is strictly Server ➔ Client (like AI streaming or stock tickers), SSE is 10x simpler and handles auto-reconnections natively out of the box! 🧠
