☕ Gyaan Ki Baat #1: The "Degree vs. Skills" Reality Check 💭

Student: "A single sheet of paper can’t decide my future." 😎
Placement Portal: "60% aggregate & 0 active backlogs required to apply." 👀

Har quote har situation mein fit nahi hota. 

Here is a quick reality check every college student needs to hear:

---

1️⃣ Degree Matters 📜
Skills get you the job, but a degree gets you through the initial ATS filters & eligibility criteria.

2️⃣ CGPA / Marks Matter (More than you think) 📈
Most campus recruitment drives use CGPA cutoffs (6.0+, 7.0+, or 60%+) simply to filter down thousands of applicants.

3️⃣ Backlogs Definitely Matter ⚠️
Having active backlogs can instantly disqualify you from campus placements, no matter how good your coding or technical skills are.

---

💡 The Roadmap:
• If you’re in college, focus on your core academics first.
• If you have an active backlog, clear it on top priority.
• Complete your degree, build strong projects, and UP-SKILL simultaneously. 🚀

Gyaan:
A single sheet of paper may not decide your entire life... 
But ignoring your degree can definitely make your entry into the corporate world 10x harder. ☕
