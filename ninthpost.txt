☕ Gyaan Ki Baat: What Happens When You Pay by Card on Zomato? 💳🍔
You enter CVV, submit OTP, and in 2.5 seconds: "Order Confirmed!" 🍕
Here is the high-speed fintech flow behind the scenes:

🛡️ Card Tokenization: Merchants NEVER store your 16-digit card number. Networks issue an encrypted device token.
🧠 3D Secure 2.0: AI scores risk in real-time. Low risk = frictionless instant checkout; high risk = OTP challenge.
🔄 Idempotency Keys: If internet drops right after submitting OTP, your retry sends a unique UUID to prevent double-billing.
⏳ Auth vs. Settlement: Credit limit is locked instantly, but funds settle to merchant 24-48 hours later.

💡 TL;DR: Network drops will happen. Always use Idempotency Keys and Webhooks with retry queues! ⚡
