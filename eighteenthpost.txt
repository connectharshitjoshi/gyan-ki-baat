☕ Gyaan Ki Baat: How Google Maps Finds the Shortest Path in 30ms (Why Dijkstra & A* Fail at Scale) 🗺️🚗⚡

You type "Delhi to Bengaluru" (2,170 km) or "New York to Los Angeles" (4,500 km) and hit Navigate.

In less than 30 milliseconds, Google Maps:
• Calculates the turn-by-turn route across 100M+ road segments.
• Predicts your ETA down to the minute (accounting for traffic jams 500 km away).
• Suggests 2 alternate routes (+15m, +40m) with toll savings.

If Google ran classic Dijkstra or BFS taught in computer science textbooks, exploring a 2,000 km journey would touch 25+ Million nodes, burn seconds of CPU, and crash under 50,000 requests/sec. ❌

Here is how Google Maps routes across continents in under 30 milliseconds: 🧠👇

1️⃣ The Road Network is NOT a Simple Flat 2D Graph 🌐
• Road segments have directionality (one-ways, dual carriageways).
• Turn penalties: "No right turn 8 AM–11 AM" means the graph must track transition costs.
• Non-planar: Overpasses, tunnels, and flyovers cross in 2D space without intersecting in 3D.
• Edge Weight ≠ Physical Distance. Weight = (Distance / Current Speed) + Turn Overhead + Signal Delays.

2️⃣ Why Dijkstra & A* Fall Short 🐢
• Dijkstra expands as a blind uniform radial circle. Exploring Paris to Berlin searches West towards Normandy and South towards Lyon with equal priority (wasting 95% CPU!).
• A* uses straight-line Euclidean distance heuristic h(n) to pull the search toward the target in an ellipse.
⚠️ But A* still visits hundreds of thousands of residential alleys along the corridor (~800ms–2,000ms latency). Still too slow!

3️⃣ The Secret Weapon: Contraction Hierarchies (CH) 🚀
When driving cross-country, do you contemplate turning into a suburban alley in an intermediate city? No! You take local roads to the highway, cruise 1,000 km, and only exit to local streets at your destination.
• Preprocessing: Low-rank residential intersections are "contracted" (removed). If a removed node was part of the unique shortest path, a direct "Shortcut Edge" is inserted between its neighbors.
• Query Time: Bidirectional Dijkstra searches UPWARD from Source and UPWARD from Destination.
• Result: They meet at the highest-rank highway peak in < 3ms, visiting under 500 nodes instead of 25 Million! (~3,400x speedup!).

4️⃣ Live Traffic & Customizable Route Planning (CRP) ⏱️
Vanilla CH takes hours to precompute. But traffic speeds change every 30 seconds!
Google Maps decouples:
1. Static Metric-Independent Topology (road shapes, turns).
2. Dynamic Metric Customization (live speeds, accidents, lane closures).
Using multi-level cell partitioning, live traffic weights update in memory across entire cities in under 1 second!

5️⃣ Next-Gen ETAs: DeepMind Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) 🤖
Static speed calculations fail because traffic evolves dynamically over a 2-hour drive.
Google collaborated with Google DeepMind to model road networks into dynamic graph "Supersegments." DeepMind GNNs forecast spatiotemporal traffic ripple effects 10 to 60 minutes into the future — slashing negative ETA prediction errors by up to 50%!
