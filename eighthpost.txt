☕ Gyaan Ki Baat: How Instagram Scales to 100M+ Users 🚀📱
When you post a photo: Pushed to 300 friends in milliseconds.
When Cristiano Ronaldo posts to 600M followers: Pushing 600M database writes would melt the servers! 🔥

How do social media feeds handle this? The Hybrid Model:
🔹 Regular Users (Push): Post is instantly written into followers' pre-computed Redis feeds ($O(1)$ fast reads).
🔹 Celebrities (Pull): Posts aren't pushed, they're dynamically merged only when you open the app!
🗄️ Database Sharding: User data is hashed across DB nodes via `MurmurHash(userId) % TotalShards` (zero cross-node joins).

💡 TL;DR: At scale, you don't query a DB for feeds. You serve pre-computed timelines out of Redis RAM in <5ms! 🧠
